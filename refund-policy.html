Payment Guidelines

School Fee Payment Policy

  • All fees paid through the online payment gateway are final and non-refundable.
  • Parents are requested to verify student details and fee amount carefully before making payment.
  • Fees are payable only in 4 fixed installments as per the school fee structure.
  • Installment amounts and schedules cannot be changed or altered.
  • A late fee of ₹300/- will automatically be added after the due date.
