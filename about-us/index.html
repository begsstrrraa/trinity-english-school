Our Story & Heritage

Nurturing generations of learners

Welcome to Trinity English School — a place where education, values, and excellence come together to shape young minds and build successful futures.

Established in 1996, Trinity English School was founded by a visionary group of educationists: Richard D'Silva, Ivan Benson, Olivia Rajah, (Late) Maqsood Ali Khan, and (Late) Alka Gore. United by a common vision to provide quality education and strong moral values, they laid the foundation of an institution dedicated to nurturing generations of learners.

Managed by Progressive Education Trust and registered under the Public Charity Trust Act, 1950, the school began its journey in a small rented flat at Meera Queen Co-operative Housing Society, Kashimira, Thane, with only 25 students. Through dedication, perseverance, and the trust of parents and the community, the school has grown steadily into a well-recognized educational institution. Today, Trinity English School proudly holds Government Recognition with School Index No. 16.16.057.

For nearly three decades, Trinity English School has remained committed to providing a holistic education that balances academic excellence with character development. We believe that every child possesses unique talents and potential, and our mission is to nurture these abilities in a supportive, safe, and inspiring environment.

Our experienced educators encourage curiosity, critical thinking, creativity, leadership, and lifelong learning. Through innovative teaching practices, co-curricular activities, value-based education, and personal attention, we strive to develop confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals who are prepared to succeed in an ever-changing world.

Guided by our motto of excellence, integrity, and service, Trinity English School continues to empower students to become productive citizens and future leaders. The legacy of our founders inspires us to uphold the highest standards of education while fostering a culture of respect, inclusivity, and continuous growth.

Trinity English School is more than an educational institution—it is a vibrant learning community where knowledge is nurtured, values are strengthened, talents are discovered, and dreams are transformed into achievements. We warmly invite students, parents, and well-wishers to join the Trinity family and be a part of our continuing journey of learning, growth, and excellence.
