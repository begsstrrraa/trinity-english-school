About us

A school that has grown slowly and steadily

Trinity English School was founded in 1996 by Mr. Richard D’Silva and visionary educationists. The school is managed by Progressive Education Trust and is registered under the Public Charity Trust Act 1950.

Starting in a small rental flat in Meera Queen Co-operative Housing Society, Kashimira, Thane with just 25 students, the school has grown steadily into a recognized institution of excellence.

  • Government Recognized School (Index No. 16.16.057)
  • Managed by Progressive Education Trust
  • Holistic Education & Strong Moral Values
