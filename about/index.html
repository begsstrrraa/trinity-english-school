Our Story

A school that has grown slowly and steadily

Trinity English School was founded in the year 1996 by visionary educationists Mr. Richard D’Silva, Mr. Ivan Benson, Mrs. Olivia Rajah, (Late) Mr. Maqsood Ali Khan, and (Late) Mrs. Alka Gore.

The school is managed by Progressive Education Trust and is registered under the Public Charity Trust Act 1950.

Starting in a small rental flat in Meera Queen Co-operative Housing Society, Kashimira, Thane with just 25 students, the school has grown steadily into a recognized institution of excellence.

The school has expanded step-by-step with dedication and commitment:

  • Primary section started in June 1997
  • Secondary section added in 2002
  • Received Government Recognition from Maharashtra State Education Board in 2006 (School Index No. 16.16.057)
  • First SSC Board batch appeared in March 2007 with 100% outstanding results

Our commitment remains rooted in holistic education, strong moral character, and creating a joyful learning atmosphere where every child is empowered to reach their true potential.
